# Foundation review · 0.1.5

Reviewed on Windows with Godot 4.7.2 .NET, Forward+ and Intel Graphics.

- The C# build completed with zero warnings and zero errors. All runtime audio, character rigs and expression poses imported successfully.
- 113 unit cases passed, including presentation preference recovery, ignored legacy automatic-checkout fields, bounded social gesture blending, mouth-envelope interpolation, and hash/format/duration/level checks on all 28 runtime WAVs and 20 vocal envelopes.
- 84 headless assertions passed against the real controller and customers. Four purchases produced $424 with settled cash, stock and wholesale cost reconciled. Waiting lines did not move the owner either before her first checkout command or after her walking task. Explicit C/checkout commands resumed service. The current-customer-first, newest-command and physical-restock/save/load rules remained covered. Opening or closing a paused store resumed audio and tasks together.
- Real arrival, checkout, pickup, placement, voice and footstep events dispatched through the production audio controller. Pause stopped both tasks and audio. Fullscreen/mute preferences round-tripped in an isolated test location without changing settled store data.
- Speech timing and mouth sampling now live in `PersonVoice`, separate from skeleton animation. Audio cues are preloaded; fixed-channel selection and speaker checks avoid LINQ iterator allocations, and music ducking reuses cached bus indexes. Simultaneous reactions remained limited to two voices, paused mouths stayed fixed, footstep bursts stayed bounded and checkout cues played despite occupied channels.
- Forward+ review showed the new greeting wave, warmer smile and idle stance, head movement and conversational gestures on the original skinned characters. A real customer bought a chair; the owner physically collected and delivered one replacement, then stayed beside the display while customers waited in line.
- Native fullscreen filled the display; returning to windowed mode restored its bounds. The Audio dialog's mute control persisted correctly, and M unmuted it. Low-contrast audio headings were corrected after screenshot review. The packaged Windows game loaded the existing store, displayed readable audio controls, restored its window after F11 and closed cleanly; the player save's SHA-256 stayed unchanged.
- Store effects and the quiet swung theme use original procedural synthesis. Fictional female/male vocal bites use credited generic Kokoro voices, with model URLs/hashes, output hashes and the model license preserved. Neural tools and weights remain development-only. No EA/The Sims recordings or dialogue are included.
- Audio shutdown stops players and lets the audio thread drain before closing. Automated checks and the native review closed without resource errors. Test saves/preferences stay in `.godot/test-results`; captures and model inputs stay under ignored `.tools`. Debug checks/previews are excluded from release builds.

Animation remains procedural, with amplitude-based mouth movement rather than phoneme-level facial animation. Crowded custom layouts, other audio devices and other GPUs need broader playtesting. See GAMEPLAY.md for current controls and limits.
