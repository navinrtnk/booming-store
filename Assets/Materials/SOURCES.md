# Material asset sources

The following 4K albedo, OpenGL normal and roughness JPEG maps are distributed under [Poly Haven's CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license):

- [White Oak Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_oak_veneer)
- [Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor)
- [Marble 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01)
- [White Plaster 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02)

Exact original URLs and SHA-256 digests are recorded in `sources.json`. These files were reused from the owner's Bartender Specialogist project with their original CC0 provenance. `scripts/fetch_materials.py` can retrieve and verify the sets again. Their photographic content is third-party CC0 material, not exclusive original artwork of Booming Store.
